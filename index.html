ABOUT SHASHTI DENTAL CLINIC

Pioneering Dental & Orthodontic Healthcare in Tirupur

Established in 2012, Shashti Dental Clinic & Orthodontic Centre has been a trusted cornerstone of high-quality dental care in Periyar Colony, Tirupur. Conveniently situated opposite BM Ortho Hospital and near Dhamu Large, our modern facility serves patients from across Tirupur and nearby districts.

Our philosophy centers around patient comfort, transparent pricing, and gentle, stress-free clinical procedures. Whether you need orthodontic teeth alignment, cosmetic smile enhancement, or routine oral maintenance, our experienced team ensures exceptional clinical outcomes.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about visiting Shashti Dental Clinic.

How can I book an appointment at Shashti Dental Clinic?
You can easily book online using the "Book Appointment" button on this website, or call our direct helpline at +91 96779 36740. Walk-ins are also welcome during clinic operating hours.
What are the operating hours of the clinic?
The clinic operates Monday through Saturday from 10:00 AM to 8:00 PM, and on Sundays from 10:00 AM to 1:30 PM.
Is Root Canal Treatment (RCT) painful?
No! With modern local anesthetics and precision rotary endodontic equipment at Shashti Dental Clinic, root canal treatments are virtually painless and save your natural tooth.
What payment methods are accepted?
We accept Cash, UPI payments (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), and major debit/credit cards for patient convenience.
Where is the clinic located in Tirupur?
Shashti Dental Clinic & Orthodontic Centre is located in Periyar Colony, Tirupur, directly opposite BM ORTHO HOSPITAL and near Dhamu Large. It is easily accessible via public and private transport.
